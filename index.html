PROJECT ENHANCEMENT

Failure Injection & Resilience Testing

Controlled failures are injected into the payment task to demonstrate AWS Step Functions retry and Catch mechanisms.

Always Fail

Payment fails on every attempt. Step Functions retries the payment and invokes Refund after retries are exhausted.

Fail Once, Then Recover

The first payment attempt fails. Step Functions retries the task and the second attempt succeeds.
